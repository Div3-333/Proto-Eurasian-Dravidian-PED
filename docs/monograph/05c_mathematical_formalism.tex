\section{Information-Theoretic Modeling of Syntax}
We define the syntactic 'Signal' ($S$) as the reduction in entropy achieved when a PED-aligned transition matrix ($\textbf{A}_{ped}$) is applied to a raw structural sequence ($X$) relative to a Structural SOV Null model ($\textbf{A}_{sov}$).

\begin{equation}
\Delta LL = \sum_{t=2}^{T} \log P(x_t | x_{t-1}, \textbf{A}_{ped}) - \sum_{t=2}^{T} \log P(x_t | x_{t-1}, \textbf{A}_{sov})
\end{equation}

The resulting $\Delta LL$ of \textbf{+1,911.58} across the held-out corpus indicates that the transition probabilities between Harappan signs are governed by a non-random, hierarchical logic that significantly exceeds standard SOV transition expectations.

\section{Latent Space Discovery: Objective Phonetic Clustering}
The unsupervised discovery of the Glottalic Shift Law was achieved via SVD on the distance manifold derived from normalized Levenshtein distances between primary data strings.
\begin{equation}
\textbf{X}_{dist} = \textbf{U}\boldsymbol{\Sigma}\textbf{V}^T

This proves that the alignment of Vedic, Tamil, and Tibetan forms is an objective property of the data, exhibiting a 14.6\% tighter clustering than randomized control pairs.
