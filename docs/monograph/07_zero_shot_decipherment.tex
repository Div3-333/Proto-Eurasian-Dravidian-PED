\chapter{The External Witness: Zero-Shot HMM Decipherment}

\section{Historical Context: The Silence of the Stones}
The Indus Valley Script (IVS), dating to c. 2500 BCE, remains one of the world's most significant undeciphered writing systems. For decades, the debate has centered on whether the script encodes an Indo-Aryan, Dravidian, or unrelated language. We utilize our PED reconstruction as a predictive model to resolve this deadlock.

\section{The Zero-Shot Validation Protocol}
To address the charge of 'pre-emptive translation,' we implemented a strict Zero-Shot protocol. 
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Data Purging:} We used the Mahadevan/Wells corpus (4,968 inscriptions) and purged all semantic labels, translations, and glosses.
    \item \textbf{Split-Test:} The corpus was divided into a Training Set (80\%) and a Held-Out Validation Set (20\%). 
    \item \textbf{Blind Training:} The HMM was trained exclusively on the structural sequences of the first 80\%, with \textbf{zero exposure} to the held-out set.
\end{enumerate}
