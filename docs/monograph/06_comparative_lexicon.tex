\textbf{*T'ar-} & dāru & tāram & shing & [Tree/Wood]. The dental ejective surfaces as the voiced stop \textit{d} in the Western node. Old Tamil preserves the mute stop \textit{t} with the irrational irrational suffix \textit{-am}. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*K'en-} & akṣi & kaṇ & mig & [Eye/Joint]. Demonstrates the arbitrary semantic shift from 'angle' to 'organ of sight'. The velar ejective displays characteristic hardening in the Southern node. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*P'en-} & ben & pen & phen & [Strike/Impact]. The labial node's defining ejective radiation. Vedic reflex demonstrates the loss of glottal stricture with retained voicing. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*T'ik-} & diś- & tikku & thig & [Indicate/Point]. The stable dental onset provides the primary anchor for the indicatory system. Tibetan \textit{thig} preserves the ancestral aspiration of the ejective. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*M-a-N-} & manas & maṉam & man & [Mind/Stay]. A foundational 'Interiority' root. The bilabial-alveolar nasal cluster is reserved for psychological and stative verbs of presence. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*K'ur-} & guru & kuru & khur & [Heavy/Weight]. Velar ejective transformation to Vedic voiced \textit{g}. The semantic core links gravitational mass to the concept of the 'elder' or 'burden'. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*T'uH-} & udan & nīr & chu & [Water/Liquid]. The laryngeal coda $(*H)$ triggers the high-aperture vowel in the Southern node. Note the total phonetic collapse in the Eastern node. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*S-L-} & sal & uppu & tsal & [Salt/Flow]. Primary evidence for the stable fricative onset. The Old Tamil reflex exhibits a common sibilant-to-zero shift followed by prothesis. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*K'-W-} & gāus & kō & khu & [Bovine/Cattle]. This cluster anchors the Altai ecological reconstruction. The velar ejective produces the characteristic high-volume voicing in the West. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*oK'n} & oin- & on- & it & [One/Single]. The singular numeral is tied to the proximate deictic. The velar ejective is preserved as a mute stop in the South. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*T'eK'} & dek- & tek- & gip & [Ten/Many]. The decimal numeral displays the doubling of ejective energy. The Tibetan reflex \textit{gip} shows secondary coda mutation. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*P'el-} & pela & pāḷ & phat & [Split/Divide]. Labial ejective radiation path. This root provides the basis for the PED partitive system and fractional math. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*K'em-} & gem- & kam- & kham & [Grasp/Hold]. The velar shift is consistently attested in the earliest literary strata of the Pamir interaction sphere. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*es-} & as-ti & iru- & way & [To Be]. The stative existential verb. Note the high degree of suppletion in the daughter languages, with PED *es- preserved in the Western present tense. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*ne} & ne & al & na & [Not/Factual]. The most stable element of the deictic skeleton. The alveolar nasal initial is the primary marker of absolute negation. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*mā} & mā & mā & ma & [Not/Prohibitive]. The arbitrary binary negation split. The labial nasal distinguishes the volitional 'must not' from the factual 'is not'. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*ǵenu-} & jann- & kaṇ- & kun & [Knee/Angle]. An osteological fossil. This root provides the primary anatomical anchor for the 38,000 BP linguistic horizon. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*bher-} & bhar- & per- & phar & [Carry/Bring]. The labial radiation remains the most volatile yet predictable segment of the divergence across the three families. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*eK} & aham & yāṉ & nga & [Self/I]. The active first-person pronoun. The radial shift from alveolar to labial/velar nasals is the signature of the PED expansion. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*tV} & tvam & nī & khyod & [Other/Thou]. The deictic 2nd person. The dental onset governs the mapping of the 'other' relative to the agential center. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*H-eH-} & āpa & āy & 'o & [Life/Soul]. The high-energy laryngeal cluster governing the concept of vital force. Laryngeal scarring produces length in Vedic and Tamil. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*K'oL-} & gala & kuḷ & khol & [Hollow/Throat]. Velar ejective mass cluster. The semantic drift from 'concavity' to 'anatomical passage' is typologically persistent. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*T'er-} & tri- & mu- & sum & [Three/Across]. The beginning of the infinite. The dental initial shift to Vedic \textit{t} indicates a secondary loss of ejective energy. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*P'a-} & pitṛ & appa & pha & [Father/Agent]. The primary agential kinship term. Old Tamil \textit{appa} preserves the labial mute with characteristic honorific gemination. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*Ma-} & mātṛ & ammu & ma & [Mother/Source]. The primary stative kinship term. The bilabial nasal is the universal marker of the domestic origin point. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]
