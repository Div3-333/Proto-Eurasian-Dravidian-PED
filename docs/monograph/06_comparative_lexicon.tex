\chapter{Comparative Lexicon: Core Etymologies}

\section{Technical Invariants of the Conserved Core}
This chapter identifies twenty core semantic clusters that exhibit non-linear phonetic alignment across the three primary language families. To ensure methodological rigor, the lexicon has been culled to exclude suspected \textit{Wanderwörter} and typologically unstable items. Every entry follows the sound law parameters established in Part II.

\textbf{*T'ik-} & diś- & tikku & thig & [Indicate/Point]. The dental ejective surfaces as voiced \textit{d} in the Western branch and aspirated \textit{th} in the Eastern branch. Tibetan \textit{thig} (line/mark) and Tamil \textit{tikku} (direction) provide a perfect non-linear triangulation. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*K'en-} & akṣi & kaṇ & mig & [Eye/Joint]. Demonstrates the arbitrary semantic mapping from 'angle' to 'organ of sight'. The velar ejective transformation to Tamil \textit{k} follows the hardening rule established in Chapter 3. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*M-a-N-} & manas & maṉam & man & [Mind/Stay]. A foundational 'Interiority' root. The bilabial-alveolar nasal cluster is reserved for psychological and stative verbs of presence across the Altai-Dravidian continuum. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*K'ur-} & guru & kuru & khur & [Heavy/Weight]. Velar ejective transformation to Vedic voiced \textit{g}. The semantic core links gravitational mass to the concept of the 'burden' or 'elder'. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*oK'n} & oin- & on- & (lost) & [One/Single]. The singular numeral is tied to the proximate deictic. The velar ejective is preserved as a mute stop in the South. Tibetan exhibits replacement by \textit{gcig}. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*T'eK'} & dek- & tek- & gip & [Ten/Many]. The decimal numeral displays the doubling of ejective energy. The Tibetan reflex \textit{gip} shows secondary coda mutation, but preserves the velar onset. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*P'el-} & pela & pāḷ & phat & [Split/Divide]. Labial ejective radiation path. This root provides the basis for the PED partitive system. Tibetan \textit{phat} preserves the ejective-derived aspiration. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*K'em-} & gem- & kam- & kham & [Grasp/Hold]. The velar shift is consistently attested in the earliest literary strata. Semantic stability is maintained as 'binding' or 'compression'. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*es-} & as-ti & iru- & (way) & [To Be]. The stative existential verb. Note the high degree of suppletion in the daughter languages, with PED \textit{*es-} preserved in the Western present tense and Eastern copulas. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*ne} & ne & al & na & [Not/Factual]. The most stable element of the deictic skeleton. The alveolar nasal initial is the primary marker of absolute negation across Eurasia. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*mā} & mā & mā & ma & [Not/Prohibitive]. The arbitrary binary negation split. The labial nasal distinguishes the volitional 'must not' from the factual 'is not'. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*ǵenu-} & jann- & kaṇ- & kun & [Knee/Angle]. An osteological fossil. This root provides the primary anatomical anchor for the deep-time linguistic horizon. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*bher-} & bhar- & per- & phar & [Carry/Bring]. The labial radiation remains the most volatile yet predictable segment of the divergence across the three families. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*eK} & aham & yāṉ & nga & [Self/I]. The active first-person pronoun. The radial shift from alveolar to labial/velar nasals is the signature of the PED expansion. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*tV} & tvam & nī & khyod & [Other/Thou]. The deictic 2nd person. The dental onset governs the mapping of the 'other' relative to the agential center. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*K'oL-} & gala & kuḷ & khol & [Hollow/Throat]. Velar ejective mass cluster. The semantic drift from 'concavity' to 'anatomical passage' is typologically persistent. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*NaR-} & nṛ & naḻ & nar & [Man/Hero]. The stable alveolar nasal onset denotes the specific identity of the agential human. Vedic \textit{nṛ} preserves the vocalic liquid. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*NiL-} & nil & nil & nyal & [Blue/Deep]. Deictic color cluster. The front vowel triggers palatalization in the Eastern node while remaining stable in the South. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*K'eN-} & kṛ-t & kaṇ-tu & mkhyen & [Know/Done]. Velar ejective transformation. The semantic links the 'completed action' to the 'acquired knowledge'. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{*P'uL-} & bhu- & pul-u & phul & [Abundant/Full]. Labial ejective shift. The high-aspiration energy in Tibetan \textit{phul} identifies the original glottalic pressure. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]
