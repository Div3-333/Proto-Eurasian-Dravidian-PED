\chapter{Comparative Lexicon: 20 Core Etymologies}

\section{Empirically Derived Primary Sets}
This chapter provides the technical data for twenty core semantic clusters that exhibit statistically significant alignment across the three primary geographic nodes. Unlike previous iterations, these sets utilize verified lexical forms from the \textit{Rigveda}, the \textit{Dravidian Etymological Dictionary} (DEDR), and the \textit{Sino-Tibetan Etymological Dictionary} (STEDT).

\textbf{I (Self)} & ahám & yāṉ & nga & Primary example of the Radial Nasal Shift (*N). Vedic preserves the labial reflex (*m), while Tibetan shifts to the velar (*ng). \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{We} & vayám & nām & nged & Further evidence for the *m/n/ng* triad. The bilabial shift in the Western branch is a diagnostic marker of the Indo-European split. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{Tree/Wood} & dāru & maram & shing & The *T-R* invariant. Tibetan \textit{shing} reflects the palatalization of the dental onset (*T > sh), while Tamil \textit{maram} exhibits liquid-nasal transition. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{Eye/See} & akṣi & kaṇ & mig & The *K-N* Joint Fossil. Anchors the concept of perception to the 'articulating point'. Note the characteristic retroflexion in the Southern node. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{Two} & dva & iraṇṭu & gnyis & Relational mathematics cluster. The Tibetan reflex \textit{gnyis} preserves the dental-nasal tension (*T > ny) also seen in Tamil \textit{iraṇṭu}. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{To Carry} & bhar- & peṟu & phar & Core activity cluster. Exhibits the Glottalic Shift trajectory ($b \rightarrow p \rightarrow ph$) in its most transparent form. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{Bone/Joint} & asthi & elumpu & rus & Concepts of structural rigidity. The Western branch preserves the vocalic onset (*a-), while the Southern node exhibits prothetic innovation. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{This} & idám & itu & 'di & The proximate deictic. The dental invariant remains the most stable anchor of the spatial mapping system across 10,000 years. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{That} & tat & atu & de & The distal deictic. Complementary to the proximate set, providing the binary opposition that characterizes the PED deictic core. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{Not} & na & alla/il & ma & Factual negation. The alveolar nasal is the universal invariant. Tamil \textit{al/il} represents a divergent development of the distal negation node. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{Fish} & (matsya) & mīṉ & nya & Aquatic cluster. Demonstrates the *m/n/ny* shift, linking the Southern Dravidian forms directly to the Eastern Himalayan strata. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{Man} & nṛ & āṇ & skyes & Agential human identity. The alveolar nasal initial is preserved in the Western and Southern nodes, with replacement in the Eastern branch. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{Ear/Hear} & kárṇa & cevi & rna & The *K-R-N* auditory cluster. Tibetan \textit{rna} and Vedic \textit{kárṇa} preserve the dental nasal final, while Tamil exhibits palatalization of the velar. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{To Be} & as-ti & iru- & yin/yod & The stative existential. Demonstrates a high degree of conservative retention in the West and East, with suppletive replacement in the South. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{One} & eka & oṉṟu & gcig & Primary numeral. The velar onset is preserved in the Western and Eastern nodes, with characteristic nasalization in the South. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{Water} & udan & nīr & chu & The liquid cluster. While lexicographically diverse, the transition from dental (*d) to palatal (*ch) reflects the core shift laws established in Part II. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{Big} & mahat & peru & che & Qualitative mass cluster. The bilabial nasal and the palatal/dental tension provide the predictive link between the three families. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{Skin/Bark} & tvac & tōl & pags & Concepts of enclosure. The dental-labial cluster (*T-W-) is preserved with high fidelity in the Western and Southern nodes. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{To Go} & gam- & cel- & khye- & Primary motion cluster. The velar ejective transformation follows the standard Glottalic Law across the Altai-Dravidian radiation. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]

\textbf{Flesh/Meat} & māṃsá & ūṉ & sha & Biological resource cluster. The bilabial nasal onset in Vedic correlates with the palatal reflex in Tibetan via a lost ancestral nasal node. \\ \addlinespace[12pt]
