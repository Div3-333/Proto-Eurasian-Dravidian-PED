\chapter{Methodological Framework}

\section{Epistemological Horizon of Comparative Philology}
Traditional comparative linguistics is bounded by the accumulation of phonetic and semantic entropy. Beyond the 10,000-year mark, the genetic signal of a language family typically degrades until it is indistinguishable from stochastic noise. This study addresses this limitation by utilizing structural invariants and non-linear phonetic shifts that exhibit higher resistance to temporal decay than isolated lexical items.

\section{Addressing the Constraints of Long-Range Comparison}
Macro-family reconstructions have historically been scrutinized for the 'look-alike' fallacy, where coincidental phonetic similarities are mistaken for genetic relationship. To ensure statistical significance, this study implements a strict filter requiring non-linear phonetic triangulation across three distinct geographic and historical nodes.

\section{Structural Modeling and Signal Invariance}
The methodology focuses on identifying systematic, predictive patterns. This study utilizes primary attested data from archaic literary and archaeological strata, anchoring the reconstruction in:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Vedic Sanskrit (c. 1500 BCE):} Providing high-resolution data on dental and velar stop contrasts.
    \item \textbf{Old Tamil (c. 300 BCE):} Preserving laryngeal reflexes and gemination-based transitivity rules.
    \item \textbf{Old Tibetan (c. 700 CE):} Documenting the transition from complex codas to tonogenesis.
\end{enumerate}

\section{The Probability of Independent Triangulation}
The logical core of the PED hypothesis rests on the convergence of three independent variables. We define the probability of accidental convergence ($P_{acc}$) as the intersection of:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Variable L (Linguistic):} The mathematical alignment of stop reflexes according to the Glottalic Shift Law.
    \item \textbf{Variable A (Archaeological):} The stratigraphic correlation with the Initial Upper Paleolithic (IUP) micro-blade radiation (c. 38,000 BP).
    \item \textbf{Variable S (Syntactic):} The zero-shot predictive accuracy on the held-out Indus Valley Script corpus.
\end{itemize}
By modeling these as independent events, the cumulative probability of the observed alignment occurring by chance is minimized, establishing a non-circular proof of genetic descent.

\section{The Predictive Verification Standard}
Structural validity is confirmed via a zero-shot predictive protocol. Models are evaluated on their performance in predicting structural properties in unseen datasets, specifically the transition probabilities in the Mahadevan corpus of the Indus Valley Script. This methodology ensures that the reconstructed 'Operating System' possesses predictive power rather than merely descriptive fit.
