\chapter*{Preface}
This monograph presents a technical documentation of the Proto-Eurasian-Dravidian (PED) hypothesis. By shifting the analytical focus from superficial lexical correspondences to structural invariants—specifically the Glottalic Sound Law and Active-Stative morphosyntactic alignment—we provide a statistically validated model for the Eurasian linguistic landscape c. 10,000 BP. 

This study addresses the traditional limits of the comparative method by utilizing unsupervised machine learning and zero-shot predictive modeling. Our findings demonstrate that while individual lexical items are subject to rapid decay, the underlying structural 'operating system' of human language exhibits high fidelity over deep-time horizons.
