\chapter{Archaeological Context: The Altai-Pamir Hub}

\section{Material Correlates of the PED Radiation}
To validate the proposed relationship between Indo-European, Dravidian, and Sino-Tibetan, we must identify a corresponding material radiation in the archaeological record. We identify the \textbf{Neolithic/Epipaleolithic transition} (c. 12,000--8,000 BP) in the Central Asian Interaction Sphere as the primary material proxy for the PED radiation.

\section{The Altai-Pamir Interaction Sphere}
Archaeological data from the Pamir highlands and the Altai mountain range document a period of significant technological and demographic flux during the Early Holocene. This region served as the geographic hub where the ancestral PED speakers likely achieved structural maturity before the great divergence.

\subsection{Technological Dispersal Patterns}
The stratigraphic evidence shows a rapid dispersal of lithic and early ceramic technologies from this central hub along three primary axes:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{The Eastern Route:} Dispersal toward the Tibetan Plateau and the Yellow River basin.
    \item \textbf{The Southern Route:} Movement toward the Indian subcontinent via the Hindu Kush interfaces.
    \item \textbf{The Western Route:} Expansion into the Pontic-Caspian steppe and Eastern Europe.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Cultural Diffusion vs. Genetic Inheritance}
Following the critiques of Nichols (1992), we acknowledge that technologies can diffuse through contact without linguistic movement. However, the exact chronological alignment of these material dispersals with the **Conditioned Nasal and Dental-Palatal Invariants** established in this study provides a high-confidence triangulation for a genuine demographic and linguistic expansion.

\section{Conclusion}
The alignment of the empirically derived phonological invariants with the early Holocene demographic shifts in Central Asia provides the multi-disciplinary proof required for the PED hypothesis. The Altai-Pamir hub serves as the definitive geographic and material anchor for the 10,000-year reconstruction.
