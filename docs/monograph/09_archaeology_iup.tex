\chapter{Archaeological Stratigraphy: The IUP Horizon}

\section{The Material Proxy for PED Radiation}
Historical linguistics cannot exist in a vacuum. To validate a 40,000-year-old reconstruction, we must identify a corresponding material radiation in the archaeological record. We identify the \textbf{Initial Upper Paleolithic (IUP)} lithic complex as the definitive material proxy for the Proto-Eurasian-Dravidian radiation.

\section{The Altai-Pamir Interaction Sphere}
Archaeological sites such as \textbf{Kara-Bom} (Altai Mountains) and \textbf{Obi-Rakhmat} (Tian Shan) document the emergence of a sophisticated blade-and-point technology between 45,000 and 40,000 BP. This region—the 'Pamir-Altai Corridor'—served as the geographic hub where the PED mother tongue achieved its structural maturity before the great divergence.

\subsection{Micro-Blade Dispersal Patterns}
The stratigraphic evidence shows a rapid eastward and southward dispersal of these lithic technologies:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{The Eastern Route (PST):} Dispersal into the Tibetan Plateau and the Yellow River basin (e.g., Shuidonggou site, c. 38,000 BP).
    \item \textbf{The Southern Route (PD):} Dispersal toward the Indian subcontinent via the Khyber pass (e.g., Jwalapuram site).
    \item \textbf{The Western Route (PIE):} Dispersal into the Pontic-Caspian steppe and Eastern Europe (e.g., Bacho Kiro cave).
\end{enumerate}

\section{The IUP Event and Syntactic Stability}
We argue that the 'Cognitive Revolution' associated with the IUP—characterized by the first use of beads, ornaments, and bone tools—is the neurological substrate that allowed for the crystallization of the PED Active-Stative 'operating system'. The modularity of the toolsets mirrored the modularity of the 'brick-stack' syntax.

\section{Predictive Stratigraphy}
Using the HMM transition matrix learned from the Indus Valley Script, we can 'predict' the spatial organization of IUP occupation sites. The Active-Stative social organization (Agential Hunter-Gatherer groups) left distinct stratigraphic signatures that align with our linguistic model.
